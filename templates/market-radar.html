Source health & coverage

A successful feed fetch does not mean complete news coverage. Stock headlines match company names; ambiguous tickers can be missed. Direct company feeds currently cover NTLA and CRSP; HBAR has announcement and status feeds. No absence of news is treated as an all-clear.

What this radar can establish

It records what this app observed and when. Sources, prices and calendar entries can be missing, stale or wrong. Headline matching may miss developments. Research priorities are not probabilities of profit, and technical or clinical progress does not guarantee investment returns.

News and alert records are retained for up to 90 days, capped at 5,000 news revisions and 2,000 alerts. The export includes the latest 50 news items, 50 alerts and 50 calendar edits. Tracking notes and calendar entries survive restarts on persistent storage. None of these notes automatically retrain the model or alter paper trading.
