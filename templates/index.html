This is a crypto adaptation. Delta is estimated from candle direction; it does not measure actual buy/sell aggressors. The author's private settings are unavailable. Results cannot enable trades or replace a learned model.

Use your actual Coinbase fee; 0.4% is an assumption. Slippage, position sizing and daily loss limits come from Settings. Downloads may take several minutes. Five-minute and coarser data cannot recreate these signals.

Exact research assumptions

UTC-midnight VWAP; separate closed 1m/5m/30m bands; 120-minute warmup; prior complete day's 48-bin volume profile. Two lows confirmed by two later candles, with at least 10% of the known session delta range between them. Enter after the 30m close, stop below the recent extreme with a 0.1-sigma buffer, exit half at the signal-time VWAP and half at its upper 1-sigma band. Targets stay fixed; stop first in ambiguous candles; four-hour time exit and 30-minute cooldown. The trend variant excludes a 30-minute VWAP fall greater than 0.25 sigma. No Wednesday exclusion or parameter optimization.

REAL-MONEY CONTROLS

Live Coinbase runner

Real orders require local opt-in, View and Trade permissions without Transfer, and a dedicated USD-funded spot portfolio. The initial live version caps capital at $500, each entry at $150, and holds one position at a time.

Current rules require at least 1.5 units of potential reward after costs per unit of modeled stop risk. After three consecutive net losses, that market waits six hours before another entry.

Pausing preserves exit monitoring. Stopping leaves submitted exchange orders in place. Missing protection, unknown submissions, or residual holdings require attention on Coinbase. Stop execution can fail in volatile markets.
