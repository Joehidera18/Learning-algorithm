Strategy rules

Dates, context and news

Leave the date blank for the latest completed, delayed candles. A date ends the test at 00:00 UTC, excluding that date. Older dates still depend on your provider’s available history. Optional context requires the latest scheduled closed bars to be present before entries; it does not add a trend rule. News filtering is available for opening-range breakout when a Massive key is configured.

Trading costs and risk assumptions

Each comparison starts with the same paper balance. The higher-cost test increases fees, spread and slippage by 50%.

Regular exchange sessions · Long-only, unleveraged paper trades · At least 400 observed candles required. The first 240 warm up indicators. Missing prices are reported; no candles are invented.

02 / INSPECT THE EVIDENCE

Backtest results

Read the later results first

Earlier → later

The first 70% of candles form the development period, including indicator warmup. The remaining 30% form a separate later test with a fresh balance.

Allow for trading costs

Fees, spread and slippage are deducted. The later test runs again at 1.5× those costs. Drawdown measures the largest drop from a simulated account peak.

Inspect every trade

Open the journal for entries, exits, fees and P/L. Boundary marks count toward account P/L but do not count as independently resolved trades.

A positive backtest is evidence to investigate, not a forecast. Repeatedly choosing rules from the later results can overfit. A passing result requires at least 20 resolved trades and positive P/L and mean R in both later tests. It never submits broker orders.
