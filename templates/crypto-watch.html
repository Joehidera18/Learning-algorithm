How to interpret this watch

These fixed rules are experimental and have no measured win rate. They can miss fast moves and produce false alarms. One exchange’s volume is not the whole market. Gaps, stale candles, missing BTC comparison data and thin liquidity block qualifying signals.

Building interest needs at least 2× median volume, positive 15-minute momentum, relative strength and a close within 1% of the previous 20-candle high. Breakouts need a close above that high and at least 3× volume. Extended warnings take priority at +5% in 15 minutes, +15% in 24 hours, or a close at least four prior ATRs above that high. Turnover is a rough venue estimate with a $250,000 daily minimum.

The watch does not place orders, run crypto backtests or retrain a model. Saved observations provide evidence for future evaluation; they do not establish profitability.
