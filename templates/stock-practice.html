How stock practice works

Market hours count

Regular sessions follow the New York exchange calendar, including holidays, daylight saving and early closes. Four-hour bars start at 9:30 ET; the final bar ends at the session close.

Learn first, compare later

Earlier resolved examples train the model. A one-day gap separates training from later historical comparisons. Frozen and updating learners start with the same model.

Keep gaps honest

Overnight stop gaps use the observed opening price. Missing scheduled candles stay missing. An open account stops if its price path cannot be reconstructed.

Long-only, unleveraged US stock and ETF simulations. Prices account for splits; returns exclude cash dividends. Buying-and-holding comparisons use price returns. Brokerage orders, short borrowing, options, margin and settlement rules are outside this workspace. Historical results do not establish future profitability.
